Where Prices Fall Below Cost

Are there specific geographic regions that consistently operate at a loss?

Finding

No region operates at a loss — all four regions are profitable — but losses are concentrated at the state level: 10 states account for 93.3% of line-level losses, led by Texas, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Illinois, and losses are concentrated where deeper discount rates are present.
